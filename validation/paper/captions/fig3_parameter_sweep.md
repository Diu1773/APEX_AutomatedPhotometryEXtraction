# Figure — parameter and observing-condition sensitivity

Synthetic APEX frames (1024×1024 px; seed
20260702; gain 1.5 e-/ADU; read noise 5 e-). Each point contains 30
trials of 40 injected stars and follows the production detection
and forced-aperture path; bars are 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals.
**(a)** The magnitude-error MAD is lowest at 0.80×FWHM
(0.051 mag). The open marker is the request constrained by the
4 px minimum radius; the median position RMSE is 0.255 px and does not
depend on the photometric aperture. **(b)** Raising the sky from
50 to 1000 ADU changes
$m_{50}$ from 18.38 to 17.06 mag and
the MAD from 36 to
88 mmag. **(c)** Broadening the PSF from
3.0 to 6.0 px changes
$m_{50}$ from 18.07 to 16.96 mag and
the MAD from 42 to
60 mmag. The detection-threshold sweep is shown
separately in the threshold-validation figure.
