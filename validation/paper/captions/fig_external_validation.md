# Figure — external-catalog and CMD validation

NGC 6811 measurements compared with Pan-STARRS 1 (PS1) and a
Gaia-transformed Johnson reference. **(a,b)** Binned residuals after a
colour-term fit and a 14.5–15.5 mag anchor. In
$B$, the faint-end changes are +0.010 mag for APEX−PS1 and
+0.022 mag for Gaia-reference−PS1; in $V$ they are
+0.024 and +0.007 mag, respectively.
**(c)** Johnson CMDs from APEX and the Gaia-transformed reference for 1917
common stars. Their clipped main-sequence ridgelines differ by an RMS of
19 mmag. The CMD comparison tests the plotted photometric
product; it does not validate isochrone parameters.
