# Figure — crowded-field photometry comparison

M5 (34 $r$-band frames, 1406 master
sources) and M13 (12 frames, 1347
master sources) were reduced with the current APEX code. **(a)** The core
source density is 34$\times$ the field background in M5 and
30$\times$ in M13; NGC 6811 is 11$\times$
by the same metric. **(b)** Zeropoint-aligned aperture-minus-PSF residuals for
683 M5 and 482 M13 stars show no trend with
nearest-neighbour separation above the 10-pixel resolution
floor. This is an internal comparison of two APEX methods; it does not test
external absolute accuracy or unresolved blends below that floor.
